Frequently Asked Questions

How is IRS interest calculated under §6621?

IRS underpayment interest is calculated using daily compounding under IRC §6622, based on quarterly interest rates published under IRC §6621(a)(2). The rate generally equals the federal short-term rate plus three percentage points and applies from the statutory due date of the tax until the payment date.

Does IRS interest compound daily or annually?

IRS interest compounds daily, not annually. Daily compounding applies to underpayment interest, including interest charged on PFIC excess distributions reported on Form 8621.

How is interest calculated on PFIC excess distributions?

For PFIC excess distributions under IRC §1291, interest is calculated on the deferred tax amount allocated to each prior year. Interest accrues from the statutory due date of the return for that year (without extensions) through the due date of the current return, using IRS daily compounded rates.

Which part of Form 8621 includes the IRS interest charge?

IRS interest on PFIC excess distributions is reported on Form 8621, Line 16 as part of the “Interest Charge” calculation. The amount reflects interest computed under IRC §6621 and §6622 on the deferred tax.
